Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Publisher: Heber Garza. This policy covers Browsebook for Android.

This policy describes Browsebook, Android application app.browsebook. Contact: mail@heberuriegas.com.

Browsing the web

Opening a website sends requests to that website and the services it loads. Those services may receive your IP address, browser information, cookies and information you submit. Website accounts, payments, forms and tracking are governed by those websites' own policies.

Your notes and browser data

Browsebook stores annotations and snapshots locally in its app storage. Ordinary browser data can include history, cookies, downloads and saved settings. You can remove notes and snapshots from their libraries and use the browser's controls to clear browsing data. Exported images and copies you share or save elsewhere are not removed when the original note is deleted.

Browsebook disables Android application backup. Files you export or copy can still be backed up by other applications or device services.

Optional AI

When you configure an AI connection and send a question, Browsebook sends it to your selected provider. A request can include conversation history, instructions, attached page text or HTML and, if enabled, a screenshot. Attached pages can contain sensitive information, including content from websites where you are signed in.

The API key you provide is used to authenticate requests to your selected endpoint. Browsebook can also request model information from that endpoint to determine context limits. Custom-provider AI requests do not require a Brave AI subscription.

Provider processing, logging, retention and charges follow the provider's policies and your account configuration. Sending a request with API storage disabled does not guarantee that a provider retains no data. Review the provider and the attached page before submitting sensitive information.

Security and browser services

Browsebook is based on Chromium and Brave. Browser features may contact search providers, website security services and component or filter update services. The Browsebook build is configured to disable optional usage metrics, Brave Rewards, Wallet, VPN, News, Talk and Sync. Websites can independently load analytics or advertising.

Device permissions

Browser and website features may request permissions such as camera, microphone, location or notifications. You can manage permissions in Android settings and the browser's site settings. A permission declared by the application does not mean every website receives it automatically.

Support and retention

If you contact support, the email address and information you send are used to respond to your request. Do not send API keys or passwords. Locally saved information remains until you remove it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android behavior. Websites and AI providers manage their own copies and retention.

Questions and changes

Contact mail@heberuriegas.com with privacy questions. Updated policies will identify their revision date on this page.
